Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

The short version: Ember has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your subscription data lives on your device and, if you enable sync, in your own private iCloud database. We cannot see it, and we don't want to.

Data You Create

Subscriptions, payment history, notes, and tags you enter in Ember are stored locally on your device. If you subscribe to Ember Pro and enable iCloud Sync, this data is also stored in your personal iCloud private database, managed by Apple and accessible only with your Apple ID. The developer has no access to it and operates no servers.

What We Don't Collect

  • No user accounts or sign-ins
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No personal information of any kind

Third-Party Services

Ember makes a small number of network requests, none of which include your personal data or subscription contents:

  • RevenueCat — processes in-app purchase receipts to validate your Ember Pro subscription. It receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and Apple purchase receipts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Open Exchange Rates — fetches daily currency exchange rates. The request contains no user data.
  • iTunes Search API (Apple) — looks up app icons when you search for a service by name. Only the search term is sent, and it is not linked to your identity.

Notifications

Renewal and trial reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification servers are involved.

Data Export & Deletion

You can export your data as CSV at any time from Settings. Deleting a subscription in the app permanently removes it and its history. Removing the app deletes all local data; iCloud-synced data can be removed by disabling sync or deleting Ember data from your iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

Ember does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
