Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: Unfurl has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your progress lives on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it and operates no servers.

Data the App Keeps

Unfurl records the answers you give each day, which flags you have collected, your streak, and your settings (appearance and reminder time). This is stored locally on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, your progress and your choice of appearance are also kept in your own iCloud key-value storage, managed by Apple and tied to your Apple Account, so they can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone. The developer has no access to it.

What We Don't Collect

  • No user accounts or sign-ins
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No personal information of any kind

Network Requests and Third Parties

Unfurl contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests of its own: every flag, map, and puzzle is built into the app. The only services involved are Apple's:

  • iCloud, to keep your progress backed up, as described above.
  • The App Store, to show the price of Unfurl Plus and to process and restore that purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and scheduled locally on your device. No push notification servers are involved.

Sharing and Photos

When you share a result, the text or picture goes only where you choose to send it. Unfurl asks for permission to add to your photo library only if you choose to save the picture card, and it never reads your photos.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove the copy in iCloud as well, first delete Unfurl from every device signed in to your Apple Account, then remove its data from your iCloud settings, under the apps that store data in iCloud. A device that still has the app will send its progress back to iCloud the next time the app is opened.

Children's Privacy

Unfurl does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
