Roadmap

Updated November 15, 2026

  1. Next

    Widget

    Today’s result and your streak on the Home Screen. See whether you have played without opening the app.

  2. Later

    Achievements

    Badges for keeping a long streak and for filling a booklet. Every continent has one of its own for you to earn.

  3. Later

    A card for a missed day

    Keep your streak going and you earn a card. It opens one daily five you missed, and its flags are collected as usual.

  4. Later

    iPad

    A layout made for the bigger screen, and for turning it on its side. The globe and the booklets get room to spread out.
